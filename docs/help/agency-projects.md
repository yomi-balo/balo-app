If you run an agency on Balo, the Agency view on your Projects page gathers every project your
agency's experts are delivering for clients in one place. It's there for oversight: you can see
how each project is going, who is delivering it, and step in whenever you'd like.

## Who sees the Agency tab

The Agency tab appears on the Projects page for agency owners and admins, once your agency has
at least one other expert besides you. Until then there's nothing to oversee, so the tab stays
out of the way.

- Experts who are members of the agency don't see the tab. Their own work stays under the
  Expert view.
- The Agency tab is never the view you land on. Select **Agency** next to Client and Expert to
  switch to it.

If you lose the owner or admin role, the tab disappears the next time you open Projects.

## What you see

Four tiles sit at the top. Each one counts the live projects in that state, and you can select
a tile to narrow the list below it. Select the same tile again to clear it.

- **Changes requested** (_Your agency's move_) counts projects where the client asked for
  changes. The number stands out whenever it isn't zero.
- **In delivery** (_Work underway_) counts projects being worked on.
- **Awaiting review** (_With the client_) counts projects the expert has marked ready, and the
  client is reviewing.
- **Live** counts all of them, and tells you how many experts they're spread across.

The list shows the most pressing projects first: changes requested, then awaiting client
review, then in delivery, with the most recent activity first in each group. Every row shows
the project and its client, who is delivering it, its status, and when it last moved. A project
with changes requested also shows the client's note. Select a row to open the project and step
in on delivery. The back link on the project brings you right back to the Agency view.

Below the list, **Completed and cancelled** holds finished projects. It starts collapsed, and it
only appears once there's something in it.

When your agency doesn't have any projects yet, the page says "{Agency name}'s projects will gather
here", with your agency's name in place, and fills in as soon as a client kicks off a project with one of
your experts.

## Finding one expert's projects

Use **Delivered by** above the list to narrow it to one person. It starts on **Everyone**,
lists **You** first if you deliver projects yourself, and then the rest of your team by name.
Each option shows how many projects that person has under the tile you've selected.

The tiles and **Delivered by** work together: pick **In delivery** and one expert to see only
their projects in that state. The filter also narrows **Completed and cancelled**. If nothing
matches, the list says "No live projects match these filters." and **Show all** clears both.

If everything your agency has delivered is finished, the list says "Nothing is in delivery right
now — new projects with {Agency name} experts will show up here." and the finished projects wait
under **Completed and cancelled**.

## When a teammate has moved on

If an expert is no longer a member of your agency, their projects stay on the list so nothing
goes missing, and you can still open and run them. A short line under their name reads **No
longer at** followed by your agency's name, so you know who the project originally came from.

## If you own more than one agency

When you're an owner or admin of two or more agencies that each have another expert on the team,
an **Agency** picker appears above the tiles. An agency with no other experts yet isn't offered.
It lists your agencies by name with each one's live project count. Pick one and the whole view,
from the tiles to the list, shows that agency alone. Your choice is saved in the page
address, so you can bookmark or share it.

## Hearing about it as it happens

Your agency's owners and admins get a notification when something happens on a project one of
your experts is delivering. Each one opens the project, with the back link returning to the
Agency view.

- **Changes requested.** A client asked for changes. The notification names who asked and
  the expert delivering.
- **Milestone over estimate.** A milestone has logged more hours than estimated. It shows the
  hours so far and the estimate.
- **Project cancelled.** A project was cancelled. The notification gives the date and, when known, who cancelled it. This one also arrives by email, with a
  **View the project** button, so it reaches you even if you aren't in Balo that day.

You aren't notified about your own work: if you're the one delivering the project, or the
person who made the change, you won't get a second notice. Agency members who aren't owners or
admins don't receive these notifications.
