Joining a consultation takes a few clicks. This guide covers when you can get in, the different
ways in and what you see when the call wraps up.

## When you can join

Join opens 3 minutes before the scheduled start and stays available until 30 minutes after the
scheduled end, or until the call itself ends. Try earlier and you'll see "This call isn't open yet
— you can join from {time}." — the time is shown in your device's timezone, and you can come back
to the same page when it arrives.

If you're signed in to Balo and you're part of the consultation, opening the link takes you
straight in. There's no name or email step.

Before you enter, you get a quick camera-and-sound check headed **Ready to join?** Choose **Join
now** when you're set. If your camera is blocked in your browser, you can still join. Tick **Skip
this next time** and Balo takes you straight into the call on this device from then on.

Consultations are recorded and may be transcribed, and you're told before you go in: "This
consultation is recorded and may be transcribed."

## Joining from a meeting link

Someone invited from outside the team can use the meeting link. They land on **Join this meeting**,
type their name and email, and choose **Ask to join**. The host decides whether to let them in,
and while they wait they see "Waiting for someone to let you in". They enter automatically once
admitted, so there's no need to refresh.

People who join from the link join as guests. Their time doesn't count towards the consultation
until someone from the client company uses **Vouch** on them. Once vouched, "They'll join as your
colleague, and time with the expert counts toward this consultation from now on." Only vouch for
someone you recognise.

![The Join this meeting form with the name and email fields and the Ask to join button highlighted.](02)

## Getting back in

Closed the page after asking to join? Choose **Already asked to join? Email me my link** on the
same page. We send a fresh link, only to the address you used.

![The Join this meeting page with the Already asked to join? Email me my link option highlighted.](03)

## Joining from an email invitation

Guests invited by email by name see **You're invited** when they open their link. They choose **Join
the call** and go straight in, with no waiting room. The same Join window applies: the link works
from 3 minutes before the start.

![An email invitation page headed You're invited, with the Join the call button highlighted.](04)

## During the call

The **People** panel lists everyone **In the call** and anyone **Waiting to join**. Whoever hosts
the call can **Admit** or **Deny** each person waiting. Balo reminds them it hasn't checked who
link visitors are, so admit only people you're expecting. Up to 10 people can be in a call. After
that, the next person sees "This call is full — 10 people is the limit."

Use **Share a file** in the Files panel to add a file. Anything you drop there stays with this
consultation. Everyone in the call can chat with **Message everyone…**, and **React** sends a quick
reaction. While the call is recorded, a notice says: "This call is being recorded and may be
transcribed".

## If your company's balance runs low

This part is for clients only; experts don't see it. The call has a balance meter, and you'll see
**Running low** when your company's credit is getting close to used up. Nothing stops the call. If
your company has a card on file you can choose **Keep going**, and the extra time settles to your
card afterwards. Either way you'll see "Your call keeps going — top up whenever you like to bring
your balance back up." If extra time isn't covered by a card, the next top-up covers it.

If you're on a team and can't manage billing, you'll see **Ask {name} to top up** instead, which
lets your billing admin know.

A call that goes well past its scheduled end finishes on its own 30 minutes after the scheduled
end. See **Buying credit** for how to top up.

## When the call ends

Anyone can choose **Leave the call**. Whoever hosts can also choose **End the call for everyone**.
Nothing is lost either way — the recap, notes and files all
stay with the consultation.

Clients then see **Consultation complete**, with "Your recap and receipt are on the way". Experts
get their own wrap-up page for the call.
