# What happens after you apply

Once you submit your expert application, our team takes it from there — here's what to
expect, and what happens either way it goes.

## How long it takes

We aim to come back to you within 2–3 business days — the submission email says the same.
Along the way, a short call with our team may be part of the process, to verify your
expertise before a final decision is made.

## What we look at

When we review an application, it comes down to four things:

- **Experience depth** — the hands-on Salesforce experience the application shows
- **Verifiable certifications** — whether the certifications listed can be confirmed
- **A complete application** — whether we have everything we need to assess it properly
- **Current client demand** — whether the skills on offer match what clients are asking for
  right now

## If your application is approved

You'll get an email letting you know you're in. The next step is completing your profile
at **/expert/settings** — add a photo, set your rate, connect your calendar, and configure
your payout method. It only takes a few minutes, and once it's done you're visible in the
marketplace.

## If your application isn't approved

We'll email you, and we'll say which of the four things above it came down to. It isn't a
judgement on your work — it's where things stand today, against what clients are asking us
for right now.

Nothing further is needed from you, and your Balo account stays exactly as it is: you can
keep using Balo to find experts of your own whenever you need one.

Submitting the same application again isn't something you can do from your account today.
Experience, certifications and the mix of work clients ask us for all move over time, so if
yours change we'd like to hear about it — write to us at
[support@getbalo.com](mailto:support@getbalo.com) and a person will pick it up.
