Once you submit your expert application, our team takes it from there — here's what to
expect, and what happens either way it goes.

## How long it takes

Once you submit, you'll see **Application Received!** and get a confirmation email. We aim to come
back to you within 2–3 business days. Along the way, a short call with our team may be part of the
process, to verify your expertise before a final decision is made.

![The Application Received confirmation, with the line about the 2–3 business day review highlighted.](01)

## What we look at

When we review an application, it comes down to four things:

- **Experience depth** — the hands-on Salesforce experience the application shows
- **Verifiable certifications** — whether the certifications listed can be confirmed
- **A complete application** — whether we have everything we need to assess it properly
- **Current client demand** — whether the skills on offer match what clients are asking for
  right now

## If your application is approved

You'll get an email letting you know you're in. The next step is finishing your expert setup in
**Settings**. There are six steps:

- **Complete your profile**
- **Verify your phone**
- **Set your rate**
- **Connect calendar**
- **Set your availability**
- **Set up payouts**

Until all six are done, Settings tells you that you're not visible to clients yet and shows which
step is next, with a **Continue setup** button that takes you straight to it. Once everything is
done, it changes to "You're appearing in search." It only takes a few minutes. The guides below
walk through the longer steps.

![The setup banner on Expert Settings, with the not-visible-yet message and the Continue setup button highlighted.](03)

- [Rate and payouts](/help/rate-and-payouts)
- [Connecting your calendar](/help/connecting-your-calendar)
- [Your weekly schedule](/help/weekly-schedule)

## If your application isn't approved

We'll email you. It isn't a judgement on your work — it's where things stand today, against what
clients are asking us for right now.

Nothing further is needed from you, and your Balo account stays exactly as it is: you can keep
using Balo to find experts of your own whenever you need one.

On your apply page you'll see **This application was reviewed and closed**, along with the date
you can start a new application ("You can start a new application from {date} — no rush") or, once
that has passed, a note that you're welcome to start one whenever you're ready. Choose **Start a
new application** and your earlier answers are still saved, so you can pick up and update them
rather than start from scratch. Experience, certifications and the mix of work clients ask us for
all move over time, so if yours change before then, we'd still like to hear about it — write to us
at [support@getbalo.com](mailto:support@getbalo.com) and a person will pick it up.
