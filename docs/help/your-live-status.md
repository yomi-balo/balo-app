Once your setup is complete and you've gone live, a small status pill tells you how you appear to
clients. You'll see it under your name in the sidebar, and a matching dot on your avatar. On a phone,
open **More** to find it. Select the pill to see what it means and what to do next.

## What each status means

- **Live.** Clients can find you in search and book you. Nothing to do.
- **Bookings paused.** You still appear in search, but clients can't book you until your calendar is
  reconnected. Choose **Reconnect calendar**.
- **Not taking work.** You still appear in search, but clients are offered a similar expert instead
  of booking you. Choose **Change availability** to turn new work back on.
- **Not listed.** Clients can't find you until the missing step is done. If your calendar has
  disconnected, choose **Reconnect calendar**. Otherwise the pill points you to the setup step that
  needs attention.

## Good to know

- **Not taking work** is something you choose, and you stay visible in search while it's on. See
  **Pausing new work** for what changes.
- Select **View public profile** in the pill to see your profile the way clients do.
- Before you've gone live, your setup checklist on the dashboard is where to look instead.
