When an expert sends a proposal, you review it on one page. This guide covers what each part
means, how to ask for a change, what accepting commits your company to and how to open the
attachments.

## What each part means

- **Overview** — what the expert will do and how they'll approach it.
- **Milestones** — the stages of the work, with what has to be true for each to be done.
- **Assumptions and exclusions** — the assumptions are what the price and timeline rely on. The
  exclusions are what's explicitly not included. Each is a numbered list. This section only
  appears when the expert has added at least one.
- **Payment terms** — the price and when each payment falls due. On a time and materials
  project it shows the hourly rate and how often you're invoiced.
- **Terms** — Balo's Project T&Cs, which are the same for every project on Balo, then the expert's
  additional terms as a numbered list, when they've added any.
- **Attachments** — reference documents the expert included.

## Requesting changes

If something needs work, choose **Request changes**, pick what needs work and say what you'd like
different. Your expert revises the proposal and sends it back as a new version. Nothing is
accepted in the meantime.

The choices are **General**, **Milestones / deliverables**, **Assumptions and exclusions**,
**Pricing**, **Payment terms**, **Timeline** and **Terms**. For assumptions, exclusions and terms,
name the item by its number, for example "Assumption 2" or "Term 1", so your expert knows exactly
which one you mean.

## What accepting commits you to

When you choose to accept, the dialog shows **What you're agreeing to**: Balo's Project T&Cs with
the rule for your pricing method, and the expert's additional terms, when there are any. If there
are more than three, choose **Show all** to read the rest. A line also tells you how many
assumptions and exclusions are part of the proposal.

You then tick the box to confirm you agree. Accepting commits your company to those terms. After
you accept, if your project has any agreed lists, they stay on the project page under **Agreed
in the proposal**.

## Opening attachments

Choose an attachment on the proposal page to open the file. Attachments are only available once
the expert has sent the proposal.
