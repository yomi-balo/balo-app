Sometimes it's quicker to talk now than to find a time. From a case you can call your expert
straight away. They get the call, and if they pick up, you're in a call together within moments.

## Where to find it

Open the case and look at your expert's card. **Call now** sits at the bottom of it. When your
expert is online, the card also shows an **Online** indicator.

You won't always see the button. Instead, the card may show one quiet line that says why:

- "{name} is in another call" — try again in a little while, or book a time.
- "It's {time} for {name} — book a time instead" — it's outside their usual hours. The time is
  theirs, not yours.

If neither line is there and there's no button, Call now isn't available on this case right now.

## The confirm screen

Choosing **Call now** opens **Call {name} now?** On a phone, it slides up from the bottom of the
screen. It shows the rate your expert starts from and the minimum, for example "From A$4.20/min ·
15-minute minimum. The timer starts when you're both in."

**When your expert is online**, the screen says: "{name} is online. We'll let them know you're
calling and you'll wait in the call room. If {name} doesn't pick up, nothing is charged." Choose
**Call now** to go ahead, or **Not now** to leave it.

**When your expert isn't online**, the screen says so: "{name} isn't online right now. We'll send a
text, but they may not see it in time. If they don't pick up, nothing is charged — booking a time
is the surer way to talk." The main button becomes **Book a time**, which takes you to booking on
the same case. Choose **Call anyway** if you'd rather try. If booking isn't available for this
expert, **Call anyway** is the main button and **Not now** sits beside it.

### If you already have a booking today

When you have another consultation booked later today on the same case, the confirm screen offers
**Use this call in place of today's {time} consultation**, ticked by default. The note underneath
reads: "No extra credit is needed for this call, and the later slot is released." Untick it to keep
your booking and make this a separate call, which is checked for credit like any other.

## If your company needs more credit first

Before the call starts, Balo checks that your company can cover the 15-minute minimum. If it can't,
you see **Add credit to start this call**. What happens next depends on your role:

- **You can manage billing.** You can top up right there and the call starts as soon as the credit
  lands. When the top-up can't be done from this screen, **Add credit** takes you to the top-up page.
- **You're on a team without billing access.** You see **A billing admin needs to add credit**, with
  the name of your company. Ask someone who can top up, or choose **Book a time** instead.

Choose **Back** to return to the confirm screen. See [Buying credit](/help/buying-credit) for how
top-ups work.

## While it rings

You wait in the call room on **Calling {name}**. It reads: "We've let {name} know. Nothing is
charged while you wait — the timer starts once you're both in." If your expert was reached by text
instead, it says "We've texted {name}". A line underneath counts down: "We'll keep trying for {n}
more seconds."

Your expert gets a text when they aren't online, have a verified phone number and it's within their
usual hours. Balo also limits how many texts one person can send another in a day. When a text goes
out, the call keeps ringing for up to two minutes instead of under one.

You can leave at any point with **Stop calling**. Nothing is charged and you go back to the case.

When your expert picks up, you go straight into the call. See [Joining a call](/help/joining-a-call)
for what the call looks like and how it ends. The timer starts once you're both in.

## If nobody answers

If your expert doesn't pick up, you see **No answer from {name}**: "Nothing was charged. {name} will
see that you called." A missed call shows up as a bell item for them straight away, and if you
haven't spoken by then they get one email about ten minutes later. Several missed calls in a row
share that one email.

If they choose **Not now**, the heading reads "{name} can't talk right now" and the message is just
"Nothing was charged." A call they turned down leaves no missed-call item or email. The same goes if
you stop calling yourself.

Choose **Book a time** to find a slot with them, or **Back to the case**. There's nothing to cancel.

## If your expert calls you

Your expert can start a call too. While it's ringing, a pop-up appears under the bell on any page,
saying **{name} is calling** with the same "From" rate and minimum as the confirm screen. It offers
**Join** and **Not now**, and **Join** also appears in the top bar. If you've asked for text
updates, Balo may also text you a link that opens the case.

Choosing **Join** from the pop-up answers the call straight away. Balo still checks your company's
credit first, so if a top-up is needed you'll see the **Add credit to start this call** screen (its
**Back** takes you to the case). While the call is still ringing, it goes ahead as soon as the
credit lands. If it has stopped ringing by then, nothing is charged and you can call your expert
back from the case. If answering fails for another reason, you see a message and can try again.
**Not now** lets your expert know you can't talk right now.

Opening the call page any other way, such as reloading it or using the top bar, doesn't answer for
you. You answer by choosing **Answer** there, and the same credit check applies. From there it
works like any other call.

Only the person being called answers. If a colleague at your company opens the call page, they see
**Waiting for the call to connect** and the call opens for them as soon as it connects. If you miss
the call, you get a missed-call pop-up that stays until you dismiss it, choose **Call back**
or open the case, plus a bell item and, about ten minutes later, one email.

## Where the call shows up

Back on the case, a call that wasn't booked is marked **Unscheduled call** in the list of
consultations. If you used it in place of a booking from earlier today, the note reads "Started
early — was booked for {time}". See [Your cases](/help/your-cases) for the rest of the list.
