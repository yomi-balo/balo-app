After a call or a finished project, you can rate your expert out of five stars and add a note. Your
rating helps other teams decide who to work with. This guide covers what appears on your expert's
profile, what you control and how to change a rating later.

## What shows publicly

Choose a star rating first. The fields for your note and how you're named appear once you have.
Under them, a line spells out exactly what will go on the profile, and it updates as you type.

- **Your name.** Your first name and the first letter of your surname, for example **Sam K.** If
  we don't have a first name for you, the review shows as **Verified client**.
- **Your role (optional).** The role you type, for example **RevOps Lead**. It's saved to your
  profile so it's filled in for you next time.
- **Your company or industry.** If you show your company, the review reads **RevOps Lead @Northwind
  Logistics**. If you don't, it shows your role and your company's industry instead, when we have
  one. Anything we don't have is simply left out.
- **Your note.** The note shows on the profile unless you send it to Balo privately instead (see
  below).

Your email address and full surname never appear on a review.

## Seeing it before you save

Choose **Preview** at the end of the line to see the review as it will look on your expert's
profile. The preview uses what you've typed so far. Close it with Escape, by clicking outside it or
by saving. Nothing is shared until you save your rating.

## Hiding your company

Under your role, the **Show [company name] on the review** box controls whether your company's name
appears. Untick it to keep the name off the review. The review then shows your role and industry
only.

- Your first review starts with the box ticked. After that, the box starts the way you left it on
  your most recent review, so a choice to hide your company carries forward.
- The box doesn't appear for personal workspaces, or when your company has asked us not to show its
  name on reviews. In those cases the review never names the company.

## Sending your note to Balo privately

Not every note belongs on a public profile. Once you've typed a note, a link appears under it.
Choose **Send the note to Balo privately instead** and the note goes only to the Balo team. It won't
appear on your expert's profile or in the preview. Choose **Show the note on your expert's profile
instead** to switch back.

## Changing a rating later

You can revisit a rating whenever you like. In Balo, open the rating again from the call recap, the
case or the project page and choose **Change** (or **Update my rating**, if you gave a low rating
and we've asked whether it has changed). Then make your edits and choose **Update review**. From
the link in your email, the form opens with your earlier rating filled in, and the button reads
**Update my review**. If you've just sent a review from that page, choose **Change my review** and
then **Update my review**.

You can change the stars, your note, whether the note is public, your role and whether your company
is shown. The review on the profile is replaced with your latest version, so there's only ever one
review from you per case or project.
