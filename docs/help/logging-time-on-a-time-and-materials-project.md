On a time and materials project, the client sees the hours you log. This guide covers logging
time, fixing an entry, what happens as a milestone nears its estimate and how billing periods work.

## Logging time

Open the project page and choose **Log time** on a milestone that's in progress. Enter the hours
in quarter-hour steps, from 0.25 up to 24 hours for one entry, and describe what you worked on.
The note is required because the client reads every entry alongside its hours. A line or two is
enough: say what the hours went on.

You can log time only while the project is active and the milestone is in progress. A milestone
that hasn't started or is already complete has no Log time option, so start it first or revert it
if you need to add more hours. Only the expert delivering the project logs time, and each entry
records who logged it. Agency owners and admins can see and manage the entries but don't log hours
on someone else's behalf.

## Editing or deleting an entry

You can edit your own entries while the project is active and the milestone is in progress. You can
delete them while the project is active, whatever the milestone's status. Use the pencil or bin on the
entry in the milestone's time log. You can't change an entry someone else logged. The exception is an
agency owner or admin, including a delivering expert who owns the agency, who can edit or delete any
entry on their agency's projects. When someone changes an entry they didn't log, the entry shows who
edited it and the person who logged it is told. Edits and deletions are recorded, and the client sees
the entry as it now stands.

One day can't add up to more than 24 hours of your entries on a project. A milestone with logged time can't
be removed from the project, so complete it instead.

## When a milestone nears its estimate

The hours bar on each milestone compares what you've logged with its estimate. From 80% the bar
turns amber, so you can see the estimate coming up.

When you log time, the form tells you if the entry takes the milestone past its estimate, and by
how much. Logging is never blocked. The first time a milestone goes over its estimate, the client
is sent a notice that says how many hours are logged against the estimate and points to the time
log. If you're part of an agency, its owners and admins also get an in-app notice. They aren't told
again for that estimate unless you raise it and then pass the new figure.

## Raising an estimate

If the work needs more hours than planned, propose a change from the project page and set the
milestone's new estimate. The client approves it like any other change. See **Proposing a change
to a live project** for how to build one. A new estimate can't be lower than the hours already
logged on the milestone when you send it. You can keep logging while the client decides. If your
hours pass the new estimate before they approve, the change still goes through and they're told
the milestone is over. Once it's approved, the hours bar measures against the new estimate.

## Billing periods

The totals card shows the hours logged in the current billing period and the date of the next
invoice. A period starts on the day the project kicked off, by your calendar, and runs for the billing cadence you
agreed, either monthly or every two weeks. A monthly period that starts on the 31st moves to the
last day of shorter months, then returns to the 31st. Each entry is dated the day you log it, in your time zone, and
counts towards the period that day falls in.
