# Your cases

**Cases** lists every case in the workspace you're currently in. A case is one thread of work
with an expert: it holds every consultation, every message and every file in one place, and it
stays open until it's resolved. Times show in your device's timezone.

Open cases come first, soonest booking at the top. Cases with nothing booked follow, most
recently active first. Resolved cases sit in a collapsed section underneath.

## For clients

The list is company-wide: it shows every case your company has booked with an expert, not just
the ones you started yourself.

Your next consultation is promoted to a ticket at the top of the list, with the day, the time and
how long it runs. **Join call** appears 15 minutes before it starts and takes you straight into
the call — you're already signed in, so there's no name or email step. Outside that window the
ticket says when Join opens instead. Join stays available until 30 minutes after the scheduled
end, or until the call itself ends, whichever comes first.

Every other open case shows as a card with what's happening next on it:

- **A booked time** — the day and time of the next consultation.
- **"Nothing booked"** — the case is open and waiting on a next step. **Book another** takes you
  to the expert's profile to pick a time.
- **"No consultation booked"** — nothing has been held on this case yet. **Book a time** gets it
  started.

Two situations put a coloured band across the top of a card, because both are waiting on you:

- **Amber, "{Name} suggested {n} new times"** — the expert has asked to move a booked
  consultation. **Choose a time** opens the case, where you can pick one of their times or keep
  the one you have. If you do nothing, your original time simply stands.
- **Violet, "{Name} thinks this one's sorted"** — the expert believes the work is done and has
  asked whether you agree. **Review** opens the case, where you can mark it resolved or say not
  yet. Only you can close a case; an expert can only ask.

The name on a band is the person who actually did it. If they work through an agency and aren't
the expert delivering your case, their agency is named after them — "Priya @ CloudPeak".

Each card also shows how many consultations have been held, how many open action items are
assigned to your side, and whether there are messages you haven't read. The row of dots is the
case's consultation history, oldest first: filled for held, outlined for booked, dashed for
cancelled.

**Resolved** holds closed cases. Each says whether it was resolved or closed automatically after
30 days without activity, and how many consultations were held. Everything in a resolved case
stays readable. **Book again** starts a fresh case with the same expert.

Nothing here yet? **Book a consultation** takes you to expert search.

## For experts

The list shows every case clients have booked with you — your own deliveries, across every
client company. It doesn't show cases where you're the client; those are in your company
workspace, which you can switch to from the sidebar.

Your next consultation is promoted to a ticket at the top, with the same **Join call** window as
above. Each card names the client company rather than a person, because a company's rights sit
with the company and outlast any one member of it.

Cards read the same way as on the client side, with two differences:

- There are no coloured bands. Both of the situations that raise one are things _you_ asked for,
  so there's nothing there to tell you.
- There are no booking buttons. Only a client can book time with you.

Where you've asked something and are waiting, the card says so — "You've asked if this is
sorted", or "You suggested new times". If a colleague at your agency made the ask, the card names
them instead, so it's clear who's waiting on what.

Nothing here yet? If your expert setup is still incomplete, the page points you at **Continue
setup** — clients can book you once it's finished. If setup is done, new bookings will show up
here and in Calendar as they arrive.
