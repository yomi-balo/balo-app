Consultations are paid for from your company's prepaid credit. Topping up takes a minute, and you
can do it whenever suits you. The top-up page is at **/billing/top-up**.

## Choose an amount

Under **Choose an amount**, drag the slider or pick a preset: A$300, A$1,000 or A$5,000. The
slider runs from A$300 to A$10,000, and starts at A$1,000.

As you move it, the panel above shows roughly how much expert time your top-up buys, written as "≈ … of expert time". It's an estimate at the average rate of A$3 a minute, so
your actual time depends on the expert you book.

![The top-up page with the amount slider and presets highlighted.](01)

## Add a promo code

Got a promo code? Choose **Have a promo code?**, type it in and choose **Apply**. If it works,
you'll see the code with "applied" and the bonus credit it adds to your top-up. If your company has
already used the code, we'll say so: "You've already used this code."

![The promo code field open on the top-up page, with the Apply button highlighted.](02)

## Paying for your top-up

You're charged in AUD — your bank sets the final rate. If your company already has a card saved,
it's shown under **Payment method**, and you can use **Change** to pay with a different one. A new
card is entered securely and Balo never sees your card number.

Your credit stays valid for 12 months from the last time your balance was used or topped up, so
any activity — a consultation, a top-up or a promo code — keeps the whole balance fresh.

![The summary on the top-up page, with the amount, the saved card and the AUD charge note highlighted.](03)

## When your balance runs low

Choose what happens as your balance runs down under **When your balance runs low**:

- **Auto top-up** adds credit for you whenever your balance drops below an amount you choose, and
  keeps calls going if it still runs out.
- **Keep me going** never interrupts a call. Any extra time settles to your card afterwards.
- **Just notify me** lets you know when you're running low, and you top up yourself.

Auto top-up and Keep me going need a saved card. Whichever you choose, a call never stops because
your balance reaches zero.

![The low-balance options on the top-up page, with Auto top-up, Keep me going and Just notify me highlighted.](04)

## If someone else manages billing

Only owners and admins of your company can buy credit. If that isn't you, you'll see the shared
**Team balance** instead of the top-up controls. When the balance is low or used up, a button
(**Ask {name} to top up**) lets the person who manages billing know.

![The Team balance card a team member sees, with the button asking a billing admin to top up highlighted.](05)
