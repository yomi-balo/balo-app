Sometimes it's quicker to talk now than to find a time. When you or your expert start a call on an
open case without a booking, a few simple rules decide what it costs.

## The 15-minute minimum

An unscheduled call is billed for at least 15 minutes, the same as any consultation. After that,
you're billed for the time you actually spend together, down to the minute. The expert's rate is
shown before you confirm.

## Nothing is charged unless you both join

The clock starts once you're both in the call room. If your expert doesn't pick up, declines or the
call is stopped before you connect, nothing is charged. The same goes the other way: if you're the
one who is called and you don't answer, no time is billed and no charge appears on your account.

## What you need before a call

Balo checks that your company can cover the minimum before the call starts.

- **A card on file.** If your company has a card saved for automatic charging and nothing left
  unpaid from an earlier call, you're ready. Any time beyond your balance is charged to the card
  after the call. While an earlier call is left unpaid, a top-up is needed first, even with a card.
- **Credit.** Otherwise, your company needs enough credit for 15 minutes with your expert, on top
  of what your upcoming booked consultations will need. If it falls short, Balo shows how
  much to add. See [Buying credit](/help/buying-credit).

If your expert calls you, Balo runs the same check when you choose **Answer** on the call page, so you
always know before you join. If you need to top up first, the call keeps ringing while you do. If it
is still ringing once the credit lands, the call goes ahead. If it has stopped by then, nothing is
charged and you can call your expert back from the case.

## Calling in place of a booking

If you have a consultation booked for later today on the same case, you can start the call in place
of it. You're billed once, for the call, and the later slot is freed up. No extra credit is needed,
because the booking was already checked when you made it. If the call isn't answered, your booking
stays exactly as it was.

To find out how to join once the call is under way, see [Joining a call](/help/joining-a-call).
