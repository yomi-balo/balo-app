Your weekly hours are saved as clock times in one timezone. If your device ends up in a different
one, Balo asks whether your hours should follow you or stay where they are.

## What the message means

When you open Balo and your device is in a different timezone from your schedule, you'll see a
message like "You're in London, but your hours are set in Sydney time". It shows your usual hours in
both timezones, so you can see exactly what each choice would do.

It only appears if you're approved, available for new work, and have weekly hours set. Moving
between places that share the same clock, like Sydney and Melbourne, never triggers it.

## Keep or switch

- **Keep your hours where they are.** Choose **Keep {city} hours**. Pressing Escape does the same. Nothing changes, and we won't ask again for that pair of timezones on this
  browser. The message may come back after about a month. This is the right choice if you're travelling and still working your usual hours.
- **Switch to the new timezone.** Choose **Switch to {city} time**. Your hours keep the same clock
  times, read in the new timezone, so 9:00 am stays 9:00 am but at the new place. This is the right
  choice if you've moved.

Either way, consultations that are already booked stay booked. If a switch would put some of them
outside your new hours, Balo tells you which ones before you decide. Nothing is moved
or cancelled for you. Your time off is read in the new timezone from then on, and busy times on your
connected calendar don't change.

## Changing your timezone later

You can switch at any time without waiting for the message. Go to **Expert Settings → Schedule**
and choose **Change timezone**.

## Times marked with a city

While your device is in a different timezone from your schedule, times on your cases and bookings
show the city after them, for example "2:00 pm (London)". That tells you the time is in your
device's timezone. Once you switch, or once your device is back in your schedule's timezone, the
city goes away.
