Your weekly schedule is the set of hours clients can book you. Set it once and it repeats every
week, with time off for anything out of the ordinary.

## Set your weekly hours

Go to **Settings → Schedule**. The **Availability** card has your **Weekly hours**: turn a day on,
then set when your hours start and end. You can add more than one window to a day.

Clients see these hours minus anything already busy on your connected calendar, converted to their
own timezone. [Connecting your calendar](/help/connecting-your-calendar) keeps that busy time up
to date.

![The Weekly hours block on the Schedule tab, with the day rows highlighted.](01)

## Your timezone

Your hours are set in one timezone. The line under them reads "Hours are set in {timezone} —
currently {time}". To switch, choose **Change timezone**. Balo asks "Change your timezone?" first,
because your hours are saved as clock times: 9:00 AM stays 9:00 AM, but in the new zone, so every
bookable slot shifts. Busy times on your connected calendar aren't affected.

![The timezone line under the weekly hours, with Change timezone highlighted.](02)

## Hours that run past midnight

If a window ends earlier than it starts, it runs into the next day. A Friday window of 10:00 PM to
2:00 AM shows "Continues into Saturday". A window that ends exactly at midnight shows "Runs until
midnight".

![A Friday row with the Continues into Saturday label highlighted.](03)

## Taking time off

For holidays and leave, use **Time off**. Choose **Add time off**, pick the dates, add a **Label
(optional)** if you like, then choose **Block these dates**. Your weekly hours stay as they are;
those dates are just closed to new bookings.

![The Add time off popover with the dates and Block these dates button highlighted.](04)

## When time off overlaps a booking

If a session is already booked on the dates you pick, Balo says so, for example "1 session is
already booked in these dates", and lists them. Blocking the dates only stops new bookings — the
sessions listed still go ahead as planned.

Choose **Block dates anyway** to carry on, or **Choose other dates** to go back and change them.

![The warning about a session already booked, with Block dates anyway and Choose other dates highlighted.](05)
