Not every conversation needs a booking. From a case you can call your client straight away, and a
client can call you the same way. This guide covers both sides.

## Calling a client

Open the case and look at the client's card. **Call now** sits at the bottom of it. You won't always
see it. Instead, the card may show one quiet line that says why:

- "{company} is in another call" — try again in a little while.
- "It's {time} for {company} — book a time instead" — it's outside their usual hours. The time is
  theirs, not yours.

If neither line is there and there's no button, Call now isn't available on this case right now.

Choosing **Call now** opens **Call {company} now?** On a phone, it slides up from the bottom of the
screen. It reads: "We'll let {company} know you're calling. They see your rate before they join, and
the timer starts when you're both in. Waiting isn't counted on an unscheduled call." They see it as
a starting rate, "From A$x/min", with the 15-minute minimum. Choose **Call now** to go ahead, or
**Not now** to leave it.

The first time you choose **Call now**, your browser may ask whether Balo can show notifications.
If you allow them, a call that reaches you while Balo sits in a background tab can still get your
attention.

Starting a call isn't checked against the client's credit. The client company is checked when they
answer, and if it can't cover the 15-minute minimum they're asked to add credit before they join.
Their balance is never shown to you.

Your call rings the person who booked the case. A colleague at {company} is never rung, and if
they open the call page they wait until the call connects.

## While it rings

You wait in the call room on **Calling {company}**: "We've let {company} know. Waiting isn't counted
on an unscheduled call — the timer starts once you're both in." A line underneath counts down: "We'll
keep trying for {n} more seconds."

The client may also get a text with a link to join, but only if they've opted in to text updates and
have a verified phone number, and only within their usual hours. Balo limits how many texts you can
send the same person in a day. When a text goes out, the call keeps ringing for up to two minutes
instead of under one.

Choose **Stop calling** to end the attempt. You go back to the case, and nothing is counted or
charged.

When the client picks up, you go straight into the call. See [Joining a call](/help/joining-a-call)
for what the call looks like and how it ends.

## If nobody answers

If the client doesn't pick up, you see **No answer from {company}**: "Nothing was counted or
charged. {company} will see that you called." A missed call shows up as a bell item for them
straight away, and if you haven't spoken by then they get one email about ten minutes later. Several
missed calls in a row share that one email.

If they choose **Not now**, the heading reads "{company} can't talk right now" and the message is
just "Nothing was counted or charged." A call they turned down leaves no missed-call item or email.
The same goes if you stop calling yourself.

Choose **Back to the case** to carry on there. There's nothing to cancel. To arrange a time instead,
message the client from the case.

## When a client calls you

While a client's call is ringing, a pop-up appears under the bell on any page, with **Join** and
**Not now**. **Join** also appears in the top bar. Choose **Join** to pick up. You go straight into
the call room, and the client is already waiting for you. There's no extra confirm step on your
side. Choose **Not now** and the client is told you can't talk right now. If your browser hasn't
asked yet, the first time you choose **Join** or **Not now** it may ask whether Balo can show
notifications, so later calls can reach you in a background tab.

The pop-up has no close button and stays until the ring ends. If you don't pick up before the ring
runs out, the client sees that nobody answered and nothing is charged. Time spent waiting is never
counted on an unscheduled call. The pop-up then turns into a missed-call pop-up, and you also get a
bell item and, about ten minutes later, one email, unless you've spoken by then. The missed-call
pop-up shows even if you're already on the case when the call is missed. It stays until you dismiss
it, choose **Call back**, or open the case again afterwards.

You may also get a text with a link to join, if you have a verified phone number and you're not
already online in Balo.

## Where the call shows up

Back on the case, a call that wasn't booked is marked **Unscheduled call** in the list of
consultations. When a client used it in place of a booking from earlier today, the note reads
"Started early — was booked for {time}". See [Your cases](/help/your-cases) for the rest of the
list.
