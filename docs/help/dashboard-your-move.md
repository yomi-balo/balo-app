Your move sits on your dashboard and gathers everything that is waiting on you into
one row of cards, most pressing first. Scroll sideways to see them all.

## What shows up

- Invitations to a project request you haven't answered yet.
- Interest you've sent where the client is waiting on a reply or on your availability for an
  intro call.
- A proposal the client has asked you to send, or asked you to revise.
- Project terms for you to confirm so kickoff can go ahead.
- Change requests on work you're delivering.
- Open action items assigned to you on your active cases and projects.
- Unread messages from your clients.
- A calendar connection that needs reconnecting. While it's disconnected, you won't appear in
  expert search.

When more than one kind is waiting, filter chips above the row narrow it down.

## Opening a card

Select a card, or its button, to go straight to the case, project or page it belongs to.

A card stays until the thing it points to is done: the invitation is answered, the proposal is
sent, the terms are confirmed, the action item is completed. Opening a card or reading a
notification doesn't clear it. Once the thing is done, the card leaves on its own.

When nothing is waiting on you, the row shows a single line instead.

## Where your projects stand

Below Your move, every live project sits under its stage (invited, interest sent, proposal, kickoff or delivery), marked your move, waiting on the client, or in progress; select one to open it.
