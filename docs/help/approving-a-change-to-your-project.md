Plans shift while a project is under way. This guide covers how to ask for a change, what a
proposed change shows you and what happens when you approve or decline it.

## Asking for a change

On the project page, choose **Request a change** and describe what you'd like to be different.
Your expert is told straight away and can answer in one of two ways: with a proposed change, or
with a plain answer if no change is needed. A project can have one change open at a time.

![The project page's Request a change box, with the What would you like to change? field highlighted above the Send request button.](01)

## What a proposed change shows

Your expert explains what's changing and why, and lists each milestone that would be added, edited
or removed, with what it says now next to what it would say after the change. If the price moves,
you see how much your price would go up or down and the new project total. Any difference is settled
on the final invoice, so nothing is charged on its own when you approve. On a time and materials
project, you see the total estimate in hours before and after the change and, where a milestone's
estimate changes, the hours already logged on it. A change can also raise or lower the cap, which is
the most you can be billed. You see the new cap and how much it moves. You're still billed for the
time logged, up to that cap.

Nothing changes until your company approves. While a change is waiting for you, the milestones it
touches are marked **Change pending** and can't be edited.

![A proposed change on a project, with the new, edited and removed milestones highlighted above the new project price.](02)

## Approving

Choose **Approve change**. The milestones and the price (or the estimates and cap, on time and
materials) update straight away, and your expert is told. Approving is a decision for your company, so anyone who can approve proposals for it can
do it.

On a time and materials project, time can still be logged while a change waits. If the time logged
has already gone past the lower estimate or cap the change would set, or has been logged on a
milestone the change would remove, the approval doesn't go through and nothing changes. You can
decline it, and your expert can propose a different change.

![A proposed change waiting on you, with the Approve change button highlighted below the new project price.](03)

## Declining

Choose **Decline** and give a reason, which your expert reads. Declining leaves the plan and the
price exactly as they were, and your expert can propose a different change if that helps.

![A proposed change being declined, with the Why are you declining? field highlighted above the Decline change button.](04)

## Answered requests

If your expert answers your request without proposing a change, it appears as **Answered**
with their reply. The plan and price stay as they were. You can ask again whenever you like.

![The Changes to this project list, with a request marked Answered and the expert's reply highlighted.](05)

## While a change is open

The project can't be marked ready for your review while a change is waiting on you or while one
that started from your request is still being worked on. If a change has been waiting for a while,
the Balo team may check in to help move it along.
