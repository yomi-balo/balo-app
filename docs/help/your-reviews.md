After a call or a finished project, your client can rate you out of five stars and add a note. Those
ratings build the reviews section on your public profile, which helps other teams decide whether to
work with you.

## What clients see on your profile

Your profile shows your average rating, how many engagements it comes from and a list of reviews,
starting with the most recent. Each review shows the stars, the client's note and who wrote it.

- **The reviewer.** A first name and the first letter of the surname, for example **Sam K.** If we
  don't have a first name, the review shows as **Verified client**.
- **Role and company.** If the client added a role, it appears next to their name. When they
  chose to show their company, it follows the role, for example **RevOps Lead @Northwind
  Logistics**, or appears alone if there's no role. Otherwise the review shows the role and the
  company's industry. Balo can also hide a company's name from reviews entirely, in which case it
  never shows.
- **Filters.** Once at least five engagements are reviewed and they include both finished projects
  and consultations, people visiting your profile can filter the list by type.

Every review comes from a real engagement on Balo, which is what the **Verified engagements** note
on your profile refers to.

## Ratings count per engagement, not per person

Each case or project counts once toward your average, however many people at the client's company
rated it. If two people rate the same project, their ratings are combined into one entry, so a
single busy engagement can't outweigh the rest.

## Clients choose what's public

Clients decide how much of their review goes on your profile.

- **Private notes.** A client can send their note to Balo only. The stars still count, but the note
  never appears on your profile.
- **Company name.** A client can hide their company, in which case the review shows their role and
  industry instead.
- **Email and full surname.** These never appear on a review.

Clients can also change a rating later, and the review on your profile is replaced with their latest
version.
