A consultation is a live video call with an expert, billed per minute from your company's
prepaid credit. Every consultation belongs to a case, so all your calls, messages and files with
that expert stay together.

## How to book

1. Open the expert's profile and choose **Book a consultation**.
2. On **Choose a time**, pick a slot from their open times. Times show in your device's timezone.
3. Under **How long do you need?**, choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. The longest option is capped
   at the length of the slot you picked.
4. On **Review & confirm**, check the details and choose **Confirm & book**.

![The How long do you need? step after picking a time, with the 15, 30, 45 and 60 minute options highlighted.](01)

## If your balance is low when you book

Your company's funding is checked as **Review & confirm** opens, before anything is booked. It
passes when your company has an active card on file. Without a card, your available balance needs
to cover this call plus your company's other booked calls.

If it doesn't and you manage billing for the company you're booking for, a top-up appears right
under the form, with the smallest amount that covers the call already selected. Pay with a saved
card or add a new one, and the call books as soon as the credit lands. Nothing you've entered is
lost along the way. See [Buying credit](/help/buying-credit) for more on topping up.

If you can't manage billing yourself, you'll see **Save and finish later** instead. It lets your
company's billing admins know a top-up is needed, and when you were starting a new case it keeps
your booking details on this device so you can pick up where you left off. It doesn't hold the
time, so the slot is only yours once the call is booked.

If you manage billing but you're booking for a company other than the one you're viewing, the
button reads **Switch to _your company's name_ and top up**. It switches you to that company so
you can top up there.

If the amount needed is very large, you'll see the full billing window instead. **Top up** takes
you to billing to add credit, or **Set up billing** if the company isn't set up to pay yet.
**I'll do this later** closes the window with nothing booked.

## Choosing which case

If you belong to more than one company, first choose which one pays under **Bill this session
to**. Open cases are listed per company, so the case choice appears once you've picked.

If you already have an open case with this expert, the confirm step asks which case the call is
for. Choose an existing case, or **Start a new case** for a new issue with that expert. If you
have no open case with them, there's nothing to choose and the call starts a new one.

![The confirm step asking which case the call is for, with Start a new case highlighted.](02)

## Moving a consultation

Need a different time? Open the case and choose **Reschedule**, either next to the consultation or
from its menu. Pick another open time in **Reschedule consultation**, then choose **Move
consultation**. Same length, same link — nothing else about the consultation changes.

You can move a consultation until the join window opens, 3 minutes before it starts, as long as the
expert hasn't already suggested new times for it.

![The Confirm the new time step of rescheduling, with the Move consultation button highlighted.](03)

## When the expert suggests new times

Sometimes the expert needs to move a call and suggests up to 3 new times. You'll see **Pick a new
time** on the case, and an amber line on **Cases** that reads **Choose a time**. Choose one of the
times and select **Accept**, or select **Keep my time** to stay with your original booking. If you
keep it, your original time stands.

There's a reply-by date on the suggestion, and no need to do anything before then. If it passes
without a reply, your original time simply stands.

![A case showing Pick a new time, with the suggested times and the Accept and Keep my time buttons highlighted.](04)

## Cancelling a consultation

Open the consultation's menu on the case and choose **Cancel consultation**. You can cancel any
time before the call starts. If you do cancel, nothing is charged and any credit held for the call
goes back to your balance. If the time is the only problem, **Reschedule instead** in the same
window moves the call rather than cancelling it. Choose **Keep it** to leave everything as it is.

![The Cancel consultation window, with the Reschedule instead button and the note that nothing is charged highlighted.](05)

## If an expert isn't taking on new work

Experts can pause new work for a while. When they do, their profile shows "Not taking on new work
right now" instead of open times, and you can't start a new case with them. You can:

- **Find a similar expert**, which searches for experts with similar experience who are available.
- **Get matched for a project**, and our team introduces a matched expert.
- **Send them a message**, which still reaches them.

If you already have an open case with that expert, you can still book follow-up consultations on
it. Book it from that case's page, using **Book with {name} again** or **Book a consultation**,
which opens the next open time on the case.
