Have a piece of work in mind? Tell us about it once and we'll get it to the right person. Where
you start decides who sees it first.

## From the home page or your Projects page

When you start a project from the home page, from **Raise a project request** or **New request**
on your Projects page, or from **Start your next project** once a project wraps up, we match you.
Describe what you need and our team reviews your brief, then introduces a matched expert with the
right experience.

## From an expert's profile

When you start a project from an expert's profile, the request goes straight to that expert. They
review your brief and reply with a proposal.

If you'd rather we find the right fit, choose **Get matched with someone else instead**. Our team
then reviews your brief and introduces a matched expert. Everything you've written stays exactly
as it is, so you can switch back and forth without starting over.

## If an expert isn't taking on new work

Experts can pause new work for a while. When they do, you'll see a note on their request form
saying they aren't taking on new work right now. If they pause while you're writing, the note
appears when you send. Either way, choose **Get matched instead** and we'll find someone with
similar experience, keeping your brief as you wrote it.

## Calls with your expert

Calls with the expert on your project are free, so talk things through whenever it helps. Choose
**Book a call** in the conversation header on your request page. You can book from the moment you
start talking, right through kickoff and delivery.

If your request has moved on from an expert (you went with someone else, or the request closed),
**Book a call** opens that expert's profile instead. Calls booked there are paid consultations, not
free project calls.

Once a call is booked, the header shows **Next call** with the date and time, and **Join** when
it's time. To book more, use the calendar button next to **Join**. You can have up to 3 calls booked
at a time, and once one has happened you can book another.
