# Requesting a project

Have a piece of work in mind? Tell us about it once and we'll get it to the right person. Where
you start decides who sees it first.

## From the home page

When you start a project from the home page, we match you. Describe what you need and our team
reviews your brief, then introduces a matched expert with the right experience.

## From an expert's profile

When you start a project from an expert's profile, the request goes straight to that expert. They
review your brief and reply with a proposal.

If you'd rather we find the right fit, choose **Get matched with someone else instead**. Our team
then reviews your brief and introduces a matched expert. Everything you've written stays exactly
as it is, so you can switch back and forth without starting over.

## If an expert isn't taking new projects

Sometimes an expert can't take on new work for a while. When that happens, you'll see a note on
their request form. Choose **Get matched instead** and we'll find someone with similar
experience, keeping your brief as you wrote it.
