When your expert has finished every milestone, they mark the project ready for your review. This
guide covers what you see, what each choice does and how long you have to decide.

## When a project is ready

Your company's owner gets an email and a notification in Balo as soon as the project is marked
ready. On the project page, the milestones are locked while you review, and a banner at the top
tells you who marked it ready and the date the project is accepted automatically if nothing
changes.

## Looking it over

Go through the milestones on the project page, along with any files and messages your expert
shared. Take the time you need. There's no rush within the review window.

## Accepting the project

If everything is as agreed, choose **Accept project**. Accepting confirms the delivery, and it
can't be undone. Your final invoice is raised once you accept.

## Requesting changes

If something needs more work, choose **Request changes**.

- **Add a note.** The note is required. Say what needs to change, because it goes to your expert
  exactly as you write it.
- **Pick milestones (optional).** Under the note, you can choose the milestones your feedback is
  about. Each milestone you pick goes back to in progress so your expert can rework it.
- **Skip the milestones if you like.** If you don't pick any, your expert works out what needs to
  change from your note.

Choose **Send change request** to send it. You can ask for changes more than once. Every round is
kept on the project, and your expert sees the latest request.

## After you send it

The project goes back to your expert. On the project page you'll see:

- A **Changes requested** label on the project and on each milestone you picked.
- Your note, quoted on those milestones.
- **Updated after your feedback** on a milestone once your expert has finished reworking it.

When your expert marks the project ready again, your company's owner gets a fresh notification saying
it's ready for your review again, and the review window starts over.

## The review window

Once a project is marked ready, you have 7 days to decide. We'll send a reminder two days before the
window closes. If a decision hasn't been made by then, the project is closed out as delivered so
nothing is left hanging. Accepting or requesting changes before that date keeps you in control.
