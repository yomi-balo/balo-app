# Removing a company's name from reviews

Sometimes a company asks to be taken off the reviews its people have written. The
**Show this company's name on reviews** switch on the company's details page does that
for every review at once, and you can turn it back on later.

## Where it is

From **Lookup**, search for the company and open their result, then select **Company
details**. You need the **Edit company details** permission to change anything. Without it
you can still open the page, but the controls are disabled.

## What turning it off does

- The company's name comes off every public review its people have written, past and
  future.
- The company's reviewers no longer see the "Show {company name} on the review" checkbox when they write a
  review.

A personal workspace has no company name to show, so the switch is disabled for one.

## What turning it back on does

Turning it back on only removes the block. Each review still follows its own reviewer's
choice, so only reviews whose reviewer chose to show the company will show it again.

A review written while the name was hidden stays without the company name after you turn
the switch back on, unless the reviewer edits it later and chooses to show the company. The
reviewer was never offered the checkbox, so their review is saved as not showing the
company.

## Setting the industry

The same page sets the company's industry. When a review doesn't name the company, it reads
as "{Role}, {Industry}", so the industry is what tells a reader who the reviewer is. Pick
from the active industries. Clearing it is allowed, and a retired industry stays visible on
the page but can't be picked again.

## What gets recorded

Both changes appear on the company's **Lookup Timeline**: "Industry set to {name}" (or
"Industry cleared"), "Company name hidden on reviews" and "Company name can show on
reviews again". Saving a value the company already has changes nothing and records nothing.
